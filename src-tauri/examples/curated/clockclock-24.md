# Humans Since 1982 ClockClock 24

**Workspace** · Humans Since 1982 · price on request

Humans Since 1982's kinetic clock: a grid of analog clock hands that rotate in unison to form digital digits, in a handcrafted mineral-composite housing. First shown in 2008 and at the Saatchi Gallery in 2010, reissued as ClockClock 24 with the MoMA store, and held in the permanent collections of the Vitra Design Museum, the Musée International d'Horlogerie, and Sweden's Nationalmuseum. Hand-assembled in Stockholm.
