A table lamp in two parts: a steel lighting base and a sculptural relief glass block designed by Spanish architect Rafael Moneo. The pattern and scale of the glass make it read as an object whether it sits on a sideboard, a windowsill, or the floor. A 650-lumen LED at 3000 K, with a dimmer that runs from a faint glow to bright light.
