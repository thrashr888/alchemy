# Herman Miller Noguchi Coffee Table

**Home** · Herman Miller · price on request

Isamu Noguchi's 1947 design for Herman Miller: a 3/4-inch tempered glass top floating on two interlocking solid walnut supports that meet at a single pivot, with no fasteners. 50 by 36 inches and 15 inches tall. Rounded edges, a hand-rubbed clear finish, and felt glides. The low center of gravity keeps it steady on rugs, and the walnut deepens in tone over the years.
