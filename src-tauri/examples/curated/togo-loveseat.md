# Ligne Roset Togo ® Loveseat

**Home** · Ligne Roset · price on request

Michel Ducaroy's 1973 design for Ligne Roset: a low, wide seat made entirely of foam in several densities, with no rigid frame. Each piece is hand-sewn in leather, Alcantara, or fabric, and the quilted pleats are the structure as much as the look. Still in production and still the reference for relaxed modern seating.
