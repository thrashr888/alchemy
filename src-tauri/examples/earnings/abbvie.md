AbbVie Inc. (ABBV), headquartered in North Chicago, is a pharmaceutical company built around immunology, oncology, neuroscience, and aesthetics (Allergan's Botox and Juvederm). In fiscal 2024, AbbVie reported revenue of about $56 billion; GAAP net income was roughly $4 billion, weighed down by acquisition-related charges and intangible impairments, with adjusted earnings far higher.

The central story is the Humira handoff. Humira, once the world's best-selling drug at over $21 billion a year, lost U.S. exclusivity in 2023 and its sales eroded rapidly under biosimilar competition. AbbVie's answer — successor immunology drugs Skyrizi (psoriasis, IBD) and Rinvoq (rheumatology, IBD) — worked better than skeptics expected: together they exceeded $17 billion in 2024 and kept compounding toward a combined $30+ billion trajectory, returning the company to growth a year ahead of plan. Other themes: neuroscience growth from Vraylar, Botox Therapeutic, and migraine drugs, plus the roughly $8.7 billion Cerevel acquisition, which suffered a costly schizophrenia trial failure in late 2024; oncology rebuilding around Elahere and Epkinly; aesthetics softness tied to Chinese and U.S. consumer spending; and a dividend-growth identity inherited from its Abbott lineage, supported by substantial ongoing business-development deals across obesity and neuroscience.
