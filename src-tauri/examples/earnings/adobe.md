Adobe Inc. (ADBE), headquartered in San Jose, makes the creative and document software that defines its categories: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, Acrobat/PDF, plus the Experience Cloud marketing suite. In fiscal 2024, ended November 2024, Adobe reported revenue of about $21.5 billion and net income of roughly $5.6 billion, with the Digital Media segment (Creative Cloud and Document Cloud) supplying the bulk of a subscription base that is among the most profitable in software.

The defining tension: generative AI as both opportunity and existential question. Adobe shipped Firefly models trained on licensed content ("commercially safe"), embedded generative fill and video generation across its apps, launched AI assistants for Acrobat, and moved to agentic tools — yet the stock spent 2024–2025 de-rating as investors worried that text-to-image startups, OpenAI's tools, and browser-native design competitors (Canva above all) erode the moat faster than AI features monetize. Management pointed to growing "AI-influenced" revenue and raised targets, but growth in the low double digits no longer earned the old premium multiple. Other themes: the abandoned $20 billion Figma acquisition (blocked by regulators in late 2023) leaving product-design a gap; Express as the freemium answer to Canva; steady price increases; and buybacks in the tens of billions as the primary capital return.
