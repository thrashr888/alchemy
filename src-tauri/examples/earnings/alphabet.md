Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL), Google's parent company, is the Mountain View-based search, advertising, and cloud giant. Segments include Google Services (Search, YouTube, Android, Chrome, ads), Google Cloud, and Other Bets (Waymo and other long-horizon projects). In fiscal 2024, Alphabet reported revenue of about $350 billion and net income of roughly $100 billion. Search advertising remains the core profit engine; YouTube ads and subscriptions and Google Cloud are the fastest-growing large lines, with Cloud crossing into solid profitability.

Strategic themes: the integration of the Gemini family of models across Search (AI Overviews), Workspace, and Android, and the parallel question of whether AI answers cannibalize the search ads that fund everything; a landmark U.S. antitrust ruling in 2024 that found Google illegally maintained its search monopoly, with the remedies phase — including the fate of default-placement payments to Apple — stretching through 2025; heavy capital expenditure on data centers and custom TPU silicon for AI; Waymo's expansion of paid robotaxi service across additional U.S. cities; and continued cost discipline after the 2023 layoffs. Alphabet also returns capital through large buybacks and, beginning in 2024, its first dividend.
