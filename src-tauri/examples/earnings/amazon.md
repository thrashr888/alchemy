Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN), headquartered in Seattle, spans e-commerce, logistics, cloud computing, advertising, and devices. It reports North America, International, and AWS segments. In fiscal 2024, Amazon reported revenue of about $638 billion — the largest of any technology company — and net income of roughly $59 billion, a sharp profitability improvement driven by AWS margins, advertising growth, and years of cost discipline in the retail network.

AWS, the market-leading cloud platform, generates the majority of operating income on a fraction of revenue; its growth reaccelerated through 2024–2025 on AI demand, supported by Amazon's Trainium custom chips, its Bedrock model platform, and a multi-billion-dollar investment in Anthropic. Advertising — sponsored listings plus Prime Video ads introduced in 2024 — became a profit pillar in its own right, growing to a run rate above $50 billion a year. Strategic themes: enormous AI-driven capital expenditure on data centers and power; regionalization of the U.S. fulfillment network and same-day delivery economics; robotics and automation in warehouses; competition from low-price cross-border retailers such as Temu and Shein; and an FTC antitrust suit over marketplace practices working through the courts.
