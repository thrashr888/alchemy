Apple Inc. (AAPL) is the Cupertino-based consumer technology company behind the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods, and a fast-growing Services business (App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple TV+, payments). In fiscal 2024, which ended in September 2024, Apple reported revenue of about $391 billion and net income of roughly $94 billion. The iPhone remains the largest product line at roughly half of revenue, while Services has become the second-largest segment and carries much higher gross margins than hardware.

Strategic themes from the FY2024–FY2025 period: the staged rollout of Apple Intelligence, the company's on-device and private-cloud AI features, as a driver of iPhone upgrade cycles; continued Services growth and its effect on overall margin mix; softness and intensifying local competition in Greater China; regulatory pressure on the App Store in the EU (Digital Markets Act) and the United States, including scrutiny of the lucrative default-search arrangement with Google; and supply-chain diversification into India and Vietnam alongside tariff exposure on China-assembled hardware. Apple continues to return capital aggressively through buybacks and a modest dividend, and its installed base of active devices — well over two billion — underpins the Services thesis.
