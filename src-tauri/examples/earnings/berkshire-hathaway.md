Berkshire Hathaway Inc. (BRK.A/BRK.B), based in Omaha, is Warren Buffett's conglomerate: insurance (GEICO, Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance), the BNSF railroad, Berkshire Hathaway Energy, manufacturing and services businesses from Precision Castparts to See's Candies, and a vast equity portfolio anchored by Apple, American Express, Coca-Cola, and Bank of America. In fiscal 2024, Berkshire reported revenue of roughly $371 billion and operating earnings of about $47 billion; GAAP net income, which swings with unrealized investment gains, was substantially higher. 

The defining datapoint of the period was cash: Berkshire's cash and Treasury pile grew past $330 billion by early 2025 as Buffett trimmed the Apple stake substantially and found few large acquisitions at acceptable prices — a posture widely read as caution on market valuations. The other landmark was succession: at the 2025 annual meeting Buffett announced he would hand the chief executive role to Greg Abel at the start of 2026, remaining as chairman, ending one of the longest CEO tenures in American business. Ongoing themes include insurance underwriting discipline in a hard pricing market, wildfire liabilities at the utility business, BNSF's efficiency gap versus rival railroads, and the question of what a post-Buffett Berkshire does with its unmatched balance sheet.
