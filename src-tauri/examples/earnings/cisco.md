Cisco Systems, Inc. (CSCO), headquartered in San Jose, is the world's largest maker of enterprise networking equipment — switches, routers, wireless, and security appliances — plus collaboration (Webex) and observability software. In fiscal 2024, which ended in July 2024, Cisco reported revenue of about $54 billion and net income of roughly $10 billion; revenue dipped that year as customers digested pandemic-era backlog orders, then returned to growth in fiscal 2025.

The transformative move was the $28 billion acquisition of Splunk, closed in March 2024 — Cisco's largest ever — which added a leading security-analytics and observability platform and pushed the software and subscription mix well past half of revenue, remaking Cisco's story from box-seller to recurring-revenue company. Strategic themes: AI infrastructure as the new growth engine, with orders for AI-era data center networking from hyperscalers ("web scale" customers) climbing past the billion-dollar-a-quarter mark, Silicon One chips and 800G optics competing against Arista, NVIDIA's networking stack, and white-box vendors; a deepening NVIDIA partnership for enterprise AI factories and a stake in AI-focused ventures; security as the strategic must-win, integrating Splunk with its Talos threat intelligence; steady restructuring to shift investment toward AI, cloud, and security; and reliable capital returns via dividends and buybacks.
