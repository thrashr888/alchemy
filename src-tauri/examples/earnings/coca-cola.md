The Coca-Cola Company (KO), headquartered in Atlanta, is the world's largest nonalcoholic beverage company, selling Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta, smartwater, Powerade, Minute Maid, Costa Coffee, and fairlife across more than 200 countries, largely through a franchised bottler system that keeps the parent asset-light. In fiscal 2024, Coca-Cola reported net revenue of about $47 billion and net income of roughly $10.6 billion, with organic revenue growth in the double digits driven heavily by pricing, including inflationary markets.

Strategic themes: the balance between price/mix and volume, as post-inflation consumers in developed markets showed fatigue while emerging markets carried unit growth; fairlife's protein-shake boom as the U.S. portfolio's standout grower, prompting new dairy capacity investment; marketing transformation under the "real magic" platform with heavy digital and AI-assisted creative; portfolio breadth across sparkling, water, sports, coffee, and juice, with periodic pruning; the long-running IRS transfer-pricing dispute, a potential multi-billion-dollar liability under appeal; political attention in 2025 around sweetener choices, including announcing a U.S. cane-sugar variant; and the company's identity as a dividend aristocrat with more than sixty consecutive years of increases. Coca-Cola's pricing power and geographic spread make it a defensive benchmark among consumer staples.
