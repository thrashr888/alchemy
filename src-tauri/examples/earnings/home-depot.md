The Home Depot, Inc. (HD), headquartered in Atlanta, is the world's largest home improvement retailer, serving do-it-yourself customers and professional contractors through more than 2,300 stores in North America. In fiscal 2024, which ended in early February 2025, Home Depot reported revenue of about $160 billion and net income of roughly $15 billion. Comparable sales were slightly negative for the year — the third straight year of softness — as high mortgage rates froze housing turnover and households deferred large discretionary projects.

Strategic themes: the deliberate pivot toward the professional contractor, anchored by the roughly $18 billion acquisition of SRS Distribution in 2024 (roofing, pool, landscape distribution) and followed in 2025 by SRS's purchase of GMS, a wallboard and ceilings distributor — moves that expand Home Depot's addressable market deep into complex pro projects; building out trade credit, jobsite delivery, and order management for pros; interconnected retail investments linking stores, supply chain, and digital; and the thesis that pent-up home improvement demand eventually releases when rates fall, given aging U.S. housing stock and record home equity. Home Depot funds a substantial dividend and buybacks, though leverage rose to finance the SRS deal. Results are watched as a proxy for housing-adjacent consumer spending.
