Johnson & Johnson (JNJ), headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey, is a diversified health care company focused on Innovative Medicine (pharmaceuticals) and MedTech (surgical devices, orthopedics, cardiovascular) following the 2023 spinoff of its consumer business as Kenvue. In fiscal 2024, J&J reported revenue of about $89 billion and net income of roughly $14 billion.

Strategic themes: the pharmaceutical portfolio's navigation of the Stelara patent cliff, with biosimilars arriving in 2025 and growth carried by oncology (Darzalex, Carvykti), immunology (Tremfya), and neuroscience (Spravato); MedTech expansion into high-growth cardiovascular categories through the Abiomed and Shockwave acquisitions; a pipeline bet on lung cancer combination Rybrevant/Lazcluze and the 2025 acquisition of Intra-Cellular Therapies for roughly $15 billion to build a neuroscience franchise; and the interminable talc litigation — J&J's repeated attempts to settle tens of thousands of ovarian-cancer claims through a subsidiary bankruptcy ("Texas two-step") were rejected by courts again in 2025, pushing the company back to fighting claims individually while it carries a multi-billion-dollar reserve. J&J remains one of the few AAA-adjacent balance sheets in corporate America, with six decades of consecutive dividend increases and steady multi-billion-dollar R&D spending.
