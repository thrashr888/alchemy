McDonald's Corporation (MCD), headquartered in Chicago, is the world's largest restaurant company, with more than 40,000 locations in over 100 countries — roughly 95 percent franchised, which makes the parent a high-margin royalty and real-estate business. In fiscal 2024, McDonald's reported revenue of about $26 billion (systemwide sales across all restaurants are far larger, above $130 billion) and net income of roughly $8.2 billion.

Strategic themes: the value-perception battle, after years of menu price increases left lower-income customers visiting less; the $5 Meal Deal launched in mid-2024 and the McValue platform in 2025 were direct responses, trading margin for traffic; an E. coli outbreak tied to Quarter Pounders in late 2024 that briefly hit U.S. sales and required a public-trust rebuild; international resilience with mixed results in markets affected by Middle East-related boycotts; the "Accelerating the Arches" plan targeting roughly 50,000 restaurants by 2027 — one of the fastest unit-growth phases in company history; digital and loyalty scale, with loyalty members generating tens of billions in annualized systemwide sales and a growing first-party data asset; drive-thru automation tests and a Google Cloud partnership for restaurant AI; and chicken menu expansion (McCrispy strips, Chicken Big Mac). A dividend raised annually for nearly five decades underlines the model's stability.
