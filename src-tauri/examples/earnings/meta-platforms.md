Meta Platforms, Inc. (META), based in Menlo Park, operates Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and Threads, alongside the Reality Labs hardware division. In fiscal 2024, Meta reported revenue of about $165 billion and net income of roughly $62 billion, with advertising supplying nearly all revenue. Daily usage across the family of apps exceeds three billion people.

Strategic themes: AI-driven improvements to ad targeting and content ranking after Apple's privacy changes, widely credited for the company's post-2022 recovery; the open-weight Llama model family and the pivot in 2025 to a heavily funded superintelligence lab, including expensive talent acquisition and multi-gigawatt data center projects; capital expenditure guidance that climbed toward and beyond $60–70 billion a year, testing investor patience much as Reality Labs once did; Reality Labs itself, which continues to lose well over $15 billion a year on Quest headsets and Ray-Ban smart glasses — the glasses being the first consumer AI wearable hit; Threads' growth past 300 million users; and persistent regulatory pressure, from EU Digital Markets Act obligations on ad-free subscriptions to the FTC's monopolization case over the Instagram and WhatsApp acquisitions. Meta initiated its first dividend in 2024.
