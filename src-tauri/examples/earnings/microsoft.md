Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) is the Redmond-based software and cloud company. It reports in three segments: Productivity and Business Processes (Microsoft 365, Teams, LinkedIn, Dynamics), Intelligent Cloud (Azure, server products), and More Personal Computing (Windows, Xbox, Surface, search advertising). In fiscal 2024, ended June 2024, Microsoft reported revenue of about $245 billion and net income of roughly $88 billion. In fiscal 2025, ended June 2025, revenue grew to roughly $280 billion with net income above $100 billion.

The dominant story of the period was AI: Azure growth reaccelerated on AI services demand, Copilot assistants were threaded through Microsoft 365, GitHub, and Windows, and the company's deep partnership with OpenAI anchored its model supply — a relationship renegotiated during 2025 as OpenAI restructured. To feed that demand, Microsoft's capital expenditures rose to unprecedented levels — tens of billions of dollars per quarter at the peak — on data centers and custom silicon, prompting persistent investor questions about the return on AI infrastructure. Gaming grew with the integration of Activision Blizzard, acquired in late 2023. Microsoft remains one of the largest companies in the world by market value, trading places with Apple and Nvidia at the top.
