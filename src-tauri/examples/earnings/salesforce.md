Salesforce, Inc. (CRM), headquartered in San Francisco, is the largest enterprise application software company by revenue, spanning sales, service, marketing, and commerce clouds, plus the Data Cloud, MuleSoft integration, Tableau analytics, and Slack. In fiscal 2025, which ended in January 2025, Salesforce reported revenue of about $38 billion and GAAP net income of roughly $6 billion — profitability transformed since the 2023 activist-investor campaign forced a pivot from growth-at-all-costs to margin discipline, with operating margins climbing into the low thirties on a non-GAAP basis.

The strategic bet of the period is Agentforce: autonomous AI agents for customer service, sales development, and internal workflows, launched in late 2024 and marketed relentlessly as the "digital labor" platform, alongside Data Cloud as the fastest-growing product. Adoption was real but monetization ramped slower than the hype, and with subscription growth decelerating into the high single digits, the stock de-rated even as earnings grew. Other themes: consumption-style pricing experiments for agents; the roughly $8 billion Informatica acquisition agreed in 2025 to deepen data management; persistent questions about whether AI agents expand or cannibalize seat-based software; continued buybacks and the company's first dividend; and Marc Benioff's undiminished appetite for the spotlight. Salesforce remains the bellwether for AI's impact on SaaS.
