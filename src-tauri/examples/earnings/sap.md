SAP SE (ETR: SAP), headquartered in Walldorf, Germany, is Europe's largest software company and the global leader in enterprise resource planning — the systems of record for finance, supply chain, and HR at most of the world's large corporations. In fiscal 2024, SAP reported revenue of about €34 billion, with cloud revenue growing around 25 percent to roughly €17 billion; GAAP net income of roughly €3 billion was suppressed by a €3+ billion restructuring charge covering around 10,000 positions as the company reoriented toward AI.

Strategic themes: the RISE with SAP migration — moving the on-premise ERP installed base to the S/4HANA cloud before 2027-era maintenance deadlines — which drives the current cloud backlog, SAP's preferred growth metric, up around 25–30 percent through the period; embedding the Joule AI copilot and "business AI" across the suite as both a product story and a pricing lever; the Business Data Cloud partnership strategy (including with Databricks) to make SAP data usable for analytics and AI; margin expansion as the cloud transition matures past its investment trough; European "sovereign cloud" demand as a differentiator amid geopolitical tension; and a 2024 stock run that briefly made SAP Europe's most valuable company before growth-deceleration worries in late 2025 cooled the multiple. SAP's switching costs remain among the deepest moats in software.
