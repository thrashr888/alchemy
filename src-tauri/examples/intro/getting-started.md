Welcome to Alchemy. This notebook is a working example: everything in it is a real source you can search, cite, and generate documents from. When you no longer need it, delete it from the home screen; it will not come back.

## What Alchemy is

Alchemy is a local-first research notebook. You import sources — documents, web pages, pasted text, even other Mac apps — and then work with them: ask questions in chat and get cited answers, read sources in a proper reader, and generate documents like briefings, study guides, quizzes, and slide decks. Everything runs on your machine by default. There are no accounts and no cloud requirement; nothing leaves your laptop unless you point Alchemy at a model of your own choosing.

## The layout

The home screen is a shelf of notebooks; Alchemy opens to the one you used last. Inside a notebook you get three columns:

- **Sources**, on the left: everything you have imported. Checkboxes control which sources a chat question searches.
- **The center column**, which switches between **Chat** (streamed, cited answers), **Reader** (a full document view with find-in-source), and **Ledger** (the notebook's record; more in "The Ledger" source).
- **Studio**, on the right: generated documents and notes, from one-click summaries to flashcard decks.

## Your first five minutes

1. **Ask something.** Type a question in the chat box — try "How do citations work in Alchemy?" right here in this notebook. The answer will cite the exact passages it drew from.
2. **Click a citation.** The source opens in the Reader, scrolled to the cited passage and highlighted. This is the core loop: answer, check, trust.
3. **Add a source of your own.** Drag a PDF onto the Sources panel, paste some text, or paste a URL. Alchemy extracts it and indexes it locally.
4. **Generate something.** Open the Studio rail and try a Summary or a Study guide. Documents are rebuilt on demand as your sources change.
5. **Press ⌘K.** The command menu searches notebooks, adds sources, generates documents, toggles panels, and switches themes from anywhere.

## Models: bring what you already have

Alchemy talks to whatever model you already run or pay for:

- **On this Mac** — Apple's on-device model, on macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence. Fully offline, no setup.
- **Local servers** — Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, or any OpenAI-compatible localhost endpoint.
- **Subscriptions you already pay for** — Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, and other agent CLIs run headless through their own sign-ins, and answers are captioned with the model that wrote them (and metered cost when reported).
- **API gateways** — dozens of presets (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq, …) or any custom OpenAI-compatible URL.

Whichever model answers, your sources stay put: search uses a built-in private model that runs on-device, Audio Overview episodes are voiced locally, and image OCR runs through your own local vision model. Configure all of this in Settings → Models, and use the built-in health check to confirm chat and search are working.
