You have the tour; here is what people actually do with Alchemy, and the shortcuts that make it fast.

## Use cases

**Research a decision.** Buying a car, choosing a vendor, picking a grad program: paste the contender pages as URL sources, add your own notes, then ask comparative questions — "what do these disagree on about maintenance costs?" Generate a Data table for the spec-by-spec view and record the final call in the Ledger.

**Study a subject.** Import the readings, generate a Study guide, then drill with Flashcards (Leitner spaced repetition keeps returning the cards you miss) and a Quiz. The Audio Overview turns the notebook into a two-host podcast for the commute.

**Track a market or a project.** The companion example notebook — "Earnings Reports for Top 50 Corporations" — shows the shape: one source per company, then cross-cutting questions like "which companies flagged AI capex as a risk?" Add a Stocks watchlist as a live source, schedule a periodic report, and let the notebook tell you what changed.

**Prepare for meetings.** Connect a rolling Calendar window and the relevant Apple Notes; ask "what's on deck this week and what do I owe anyone?" The Morning Brief does a version of this for you daily.

**Build a team knowledge base.** Export any notebook as an Open Knowledge Format bundle — plain markdown, readable by humans and agents — and share the .okf.zip. Imports re-index locally and skip duplicates.

## Power tips

- **⌘K** — the command menu: search notebooks, add sources, generate documents, switch themes.
- **⌥Space** — ask everything, from anywhere: a question answered across all notebooks at once, with citations.
- **/** in the composer — every generator and action as a slash command; **@** to aim a question at one specific source, folder, or note.
- **/grep** — exact-match search across sources, no model call.
- **j/k** to step through sources in the Reader, **⌘F** to find within one, **⌘[ / ⌘]** to go back and forward.
- **Select text in the Reader** to Explain it, Compare it across sources, or stage it in the composer.
- **Menu bar extra** — ask a question, add the clipboard as a source, or jump to a recent notebook without opening the main window. Alchemy keeps working from the menu bar after you close the window, so scheduled reports and syncs still run.
- **Services menu** — "Add to Alchemy" from the text selection of any Mac app.
- **Themes** — 23 of them, from Nord and Gruvbox to Synthwave '84, in Settings or ⌘K.

## For the agent-inclined

Alchemy ships an embedded MCP server (localhost-only). One click in Settings → Agents connects Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, and friends. After that, an agent can create notebooks, add sources, run the same search you use, write notes, and update Ledger entries, with changes appearing live in the app. Anything you can do in the UI, an agent can do for you: "collect the reviews for these three products into a notebook and write up a comparison" is a one-line request.

## Housekeeping

Both example notebooks are yours: rename them, add to them, or delete them from the home screen; deleted examples stay deleted. If you keep one, keep the earnings notebook; it is a good sandbox for trying every generator against real-shaped data.
